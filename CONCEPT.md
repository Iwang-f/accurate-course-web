# Accurate Course Website Concept

## Positioning

**Accurate training led by accounting practitioners, built around real business workflows.**

The reference site sells implementation and training breadth. This concept narrows the message: prospective clients are not buying software tutorials; they are buying operational confidence, cleaner bookkeeping, and a more independent finance team.

## Primary Audience

1. SME owners adopting Accurate
2. Finance and accounting teams needing structured onboarding
3. Admin staff moving from spreadsheets or manual processes
4. Companies needing private, workflow-specific training

## Core Offer

- Accurate Online Fundamental
- Accurate Desktop V5
- Private in-house or online team training
- Workflow and database review
- Post-training support

## Differentiator

Professional accountant trainers explain both software operation and accounting reasoning. Training uses the client's job roles, transaction flow, and business cases.

## Conversion Flow

1. State the outcome: understand Accurate through real transactions
2. Establish relevance: owner, admin, and finance team
3. Explain practical outcomes: cleaner input, useful reports, independent team
4. Present flexible programs
5. Establish trainer credibility
6. Invite a needs consultation via WhatsApp

## Visual Direction

- **Surface:** Decide / Learn
- **Tone:** credible, calm, professional, approachable
- **Palette:** deep accounting green, warm off-white, restrained lime accent
- **Typography:** editorial serif for authority; neutral sans-serif for clarity
- **Layout:** asymmetric editorial composition; avoid generic SaaS icon-card patterns
- **Motion:** minimal; usability and trust over spectacle

## Required Client Inputs Before Production

- Brand name and logo
- WhatsApp number and operator hours
- Trainer identity, credentials, and portrait
- Accurate certification or partnership evidence, if any
- Course formats, duration, capacity, location, and pricing policy
- Real testimonials and client logos with permission
- Refund, reschedule, privacy, and terms policies
- Domain preference

## MVP Pages

1. Home
2. Program detail
3. Trainer profile
4. FAQ
5. Contact / WhatsApp conversion

Blog, student dashboard, payment gateway, and LMS are intentionally excluded until demand proves they are needed.
